OPORD SECTION II // MISSION BRIEFING

Mission 2: "Broken Link"

Mission Type
Direct Action (DA) / Snatch-and-Grab / Tactical Interdiction
Time & Environment
1300Z | Mid-day heat, blinding sun, high dust visibility
AO / Location
Old Daabad Fort & Ruins, High Eyl Plateau (OBJ VIPER)
Primary High Value Target
Abdi 'Terror' (Senior JAS Quartermaster)
Mission Statement
ODA 3324 conducts a Direct Action (DA) snatch-and-grab interdiction at 1300Z on Objective VIPER to capture JAS Quartermaster Abdi 'Terror', recover critical intelligence assets, and exfiltrate prior to town militia reinforcement.
OPORD SECTION I // SITUATION

Area of Operations & Intel

Operational Background
Intelligence from tracking beacons recovered during Mission 1 confirms Volk Group's chief logistics broker is meeting senior JAS Quartermaster Abdi 'Terror' inside the historic stone fort overlooking Eyl town.
Enemy Forces
  • Fort Defenders: Volk Group contractors with fixed PKM positions and suppressed rifles; JAS fighters holding outer courtyards.
  • QRF / Militia: JAS militia in Eyl town equipped with technicals (heavy MGs, RPGs).
Friendly Forces
ODA 3324 ground elements operating with dedicated overhead CAS assets (MH-60 DAP / MQ-9 Reaper).
OPORD SECTION III // EXECUTION (PHASES I & II)

Infiltration & Objective Actions

Concept of Operations
A four-phase tactical assault utilizing ground vehicle infiltration, multi-pronged compound clearing, CAS-supported defense, and foot-to-vehicle exfiltration.
Phase I: Infiltration
  • Infiltrate along dry riverbed via 2x GMVs (Ground Mobility Vehicles).
  • Establish vehicle dismount at Staging Area 600m below fort cliffs.
  • Advance uphill on foot.
Phase II: Actions on OBJ VIPER
  • Breach & Clear: Multi-pronged assault on stone fort. Suppress Volk PKM nests & clear courtyard fighters.
  • Target Capture & SSE: Secure Abdi 'Terror' alive. If compromised, extract encrypted sat phone & hard drives from cellar.
OPORD SECTION III // CONTINGENCY

Phase III: Troops in Contact (TIC)

CRITICAL CONTINGENCY TRIGGER

The moment the breach goes loud, JAS militia in Eyl town mount technicals and rush up the narrow plateau switchbacks to cut off the team's escape.

Tactical Hazard

The high ground becomes a double-edged sword—players risk being pinned on the cliff top while RPGs and heavy machine-gun fire rain from below.

OPORD SECTIONS IV & V // SUPPORT & SIGNAL

Exfiltration, Sustainment & Command

Phase IV: Exfiltration
  • Target approaching technicals on switchbacks for CAS strikes via MH-60 DAP or MQ-9.
  • Execute violent disengagement down the western rock wash.
  • Fight back to staging GMVs for high-speed vehicle withdrawal.
Section IV: Sustainment & Support
  • Transport: 2x Ground Mobility Vehicles (GMVs).
  • Fire Support: On-call CAS (MH-60 DAP / MQ-9 Reaper).
Section V: Command & Signal

Primary Objective: Capture Target (Abdi 'Terror') or execute full Sensitive Site Exploitation (SSE).
