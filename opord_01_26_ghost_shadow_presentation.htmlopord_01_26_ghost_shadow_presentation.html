THREAT ENTERPRISE — VOLK GROUP & JAS

A rogue Russian PMC enterprise (Volk Group) has established a secretive maritime logistics node in the historic coastal enclave of Eyl, Puntland.

  • Cover Operation: Offshore commercial fishing and anti-piracy maritime security.
  • Smuggling Operations: High-end weaponry including Kornet ATGMs, Igla-S MANPADS, thermal optics, and encrypted military C2 equipment into East Africa.
  • Local Alliance: Brokering assets with regional warlord coalition Jabhat al-Somal (JAS) to build an Anti-Access/Area-Denial (A2/AD) network.

FRIENDLY DIRECTIVE — ODA 3324

US Army Special Forces Operational Detachment Alpha (ODA 3324 / "Viper") operates out of an austere staging base at the Eyl Airfield Plateau (FOB Nomad).

  • Directive: Locate, track, interdict, and dismantle Volk Group's footprint.
  • Strategic Objective: Neutralize threats before advanced air-defense systems become operational across the Horn of Africa.
